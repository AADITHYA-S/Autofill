HOW IT WORKS

Designed around your consent

AutoForm separates extraction, review, and storage so you remain in control of your identity information.

01

Upload

Upload an identity document or a form through the appropriate AutoForm workflow.

02

Extract & review

Identity information is extracted and presented to you for review before anything is stored.

03

Consent

Your information is added to your identity profile only after you explicitly give consent.

04

Autofill

Use your consented profile information to populate supported forms automatically.
